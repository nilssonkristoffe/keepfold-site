Cards that know when they're done

Keep until Sunday.

Give a card a last day. When the day is over it moves to the archive by itself. Nothing is deleted; one tap brings it back.

Exactly as typed.

Leading zeros, spaces, capitals. No autocorrect, ever. A Wi-Fi password that gets "helped" is a wrong password.

On the glass, on your terms.

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show only the cards you allow. Nothing else — a widget can be seen by anyone, so you decide.

Locked with Face ID.

A locked card shows dots until it's you. It never appears in a widget, and search can't find its value. A shield against a glance — not a safe, and we say so.

Your iCloud, encrypted.

Turn on sync and your iPad sees the same cards. They're stored in your own iCloud as an encrypted field — we can't read them, and neither can Apple.

Nothing to sign up for.

No account. No tracking. No ads. Made in Sweden by a one-person company that would rather be honest than impressive.

What's free, what's Plus

Free: 2 cards, every widget size, Face ID locks, folders, the archive and Recently Deleted.

Keepfold Plus: unlimited cards and iCloud sync between your devices. 99 kr a year, or 199 kr once — yours for good. Prices vary by country; the App Store shows yours.
